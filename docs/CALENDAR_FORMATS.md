# Platform calendar formats

PULSE intentionally keeps three editorial calendars separate.

## Instagram
Fecha | Hora | Tema | Objetivo | Tipo de pieza | Material | Copy base | CTA | Estado

## TikTok
Fecha | Hora | Tema | Hook | Idea de video | Duracion | Material | CTA | Privacidad | Estado

## LinkedIn
Fecha | Hora | Tema | Enfoque profesional | Objetivo | Desarrollo o producto | Material | CTA | Estado

## Import rules

- Each row belongs to exactly one platform.
- Date and time are required before scheduling.
- Excel is an import format, not the runtime database.
- Missing content can be generated by the platform agent.
- Existing copy is guidance, not content to duplicate blindly across networks.
